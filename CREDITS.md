# Credits

Starwake's own code, ships and stations are built in code. The 3D models in the hangar (`public/hangar/`) are other people's work, all released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain). CC0 needs no credit; we credit anyway, with thanks.

The files came through two npm mirrors, because the original sites are not reachable from the build environment: `@netforge/asset-library-files` 0.2.0 (meshopt-compressed copies; per-model sources from its `@netforge/asset-library` manifest) and `@anabis/kenney-space-kit-mirror` 2.0.0 (an unofficial copy of Kenney's Space Kit 2.0, unchanged).
