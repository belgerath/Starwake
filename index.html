First run · Tumbledown

Starwake

You fly the Mudlark, a battered 35 m transport with a hold full of contraband, 400 km above Veyra at over 7 km/s. Tumbledown, a huge station where outcasts and misfits trade, is 25 km ahead and a little higher, drifting closer. Within 10 km and slower than 50 m/s you can ask to dock. Time warp brings it to you. Inside, clear customs and sell the 8 crates of ashroot in your hold. After that, the Factor has work hauling crates to outposts on Tal: tap Course and the flight computer flies you there and back, and you land.

The physics are real: nothing slows you down except your own engines, which are weak for now, and gravity bends your path into an orbit. To land on Tal, brake to a stop low over the ground, then tap Hover and settle down slower than 3 m/s, level, on flat ground.
